# Throughline Roadmap

Status: accepted behavior; v0.1 public release in preparation

## Promise

Throughline keeps a person's original intent visible from rough input through
decomposition, execution, and reality-based closeout. It seeks the smallest
complete expression, not the fewest words.

## How To Read This Roadmap

- `[x]` means the capability and its witness are accepted.
- `[~]` means a working candidate exists, but an integrated witness, review,
  release gate, or unresolved Mystery remains.
- `[ ]` means implementation remains.

The bold title is the Story. Packaging work appears only inside the Story it
enables.

The released behavior is the frozen `SKILL.md` and Story Grammar. This roadmap
adds no routing authority, workflow state, or permission.

## Roadmap

- [x] **A person's original intent stays visible from rough input to approved
  work.**

  **Detail:** The accepted behavior shapes capability-led Stories, preserves
  parent intent and concrete counterexamples, separates Moves, Briefs, and
  Mysteries, requires an integrated Witness, and maps accepted behavior before
  shared seams change. Capture remains format-free, and work records never
  become runtime routing authority.

  **Witness:** The frozen skill and Story Grammar have supported repeated
  Current planning and closeout work and survived direct human review without
  reopening their v0.1 behavior. This acceptance does not claim cross-domain
  generality or a measured compression advantage.

- [~] **People can install the same Throughline behavior independently of
  Current.**

  **Detail:** The standalone package preserves the accepted behavior, moves
  the grammar reference to a portable path, replaces internal team, product,
  and people names with generic role language without changing any rule, and
  supplies one onramp, public documentation, licensing, benchmark design, and
  final artwork. Independent review and the tagged public release remain.

  **Witness:** A fresh reviewer installs the candidate into every supported
  host, verifies the accepted grammar blob and candidate skill blob plus their
  link, applies it to one realistic source packet, removes it without residue,
  and confirms the public tag matches the accepted artifacts.

- [ ] **Every Mystery states the user's unmet expectation before explaining
  mechanism.**

  **Detail:** Give each new or materially reframed Mystery a stable negative-
  form title naming the unexpected user-visible reality. Present Expected,
  Observed, Impact, and Resolved when in plain language while retaining
  technical evidence in the linked investigation record.

  **Witness:** One software model-provenance Mystery and one non-engineering
  Mystery are independently readable: a reader can identify the unmet
  expectation, actual observation, consequence, and restoring condition without
  needing the mechanism history first.

- [ ] **Durable work becomes easier to carry without losing approved meaning.**

  **Detail:** Apply meaning-preserving compression first to Stories, Mysteries,
  Briefs, and handoffs. Compare Throughline against no skill, a generic concise
  instruction, and a separately pinned reductive control only after the source
  packet has an adjudicated inventory.

  **Witness:** Every approved requirement, counterexample, uncertainty, and
  prohibited claim remains recoverable; no intent is invented; independent
  readers identify the promise, open decision, and restoring condition; only
  then do token density and comprehension time count.

- [ ] **Each person organizes work with the grammar their responsibility
  requires.**

  **Detail:** Test a small shape-general core around recipient value,
  hierarchy, expectation/reality gaps, and restoring Witnesses, plus explicit
  grammars for software, design and organizational research, report research
  and writing, communications, and other proven work. Do not load
  every domain's stages and evidence catalog into every role.

  **Witness:** Matched cross-domain work preserves each source packet's meaning
  while loading less irrelevant context and introducing no domain-shaped errors.
  Routing accuracy, context cost, duplicated guidance, and role performance
  decide whether specialization is a reference or a separate skill.

- [ ] **Workers receive the operational grammar without carrying unnecessary
  provenance.**

  **Detail:** Separate present operating guidance from product direction and
  historical provenance only if the split materially reduces loaded context.
  Contributors and reviewers must still recover the complete accepted source
  and authority chain.

  **Witness:** A prompt inventory shows a meaningful context reduction, worker
  decisions remain equivalent on a matched corpus, every reference resolves,
  and a reviewer can reconstruct why each normative rule exists.

- [ ] **Current can import an exact Throughline release without silently
  drifting.**

  **Detail:** Current consumes one tagged standalone skill and grammar through a
  reviewed manifest and deterministic sync path rather than following a
  floating branch or maintaining an unverified copy.

  **Witness:** A controlled update reproduces both tagged blobs byte-for-byte,
  shows the intended diff before review, and fails closed on a missing grammar,
  broken link, or unexpected behavior drift.

## Domain Scope And Specialization

The first release is honestly specialized for software-development
requirements work. It assumes a requirements owner, an implementing team,
shared production seams, and software proof practices. Principles may generalize, but the current
wording and evidence do not prove a universal work grammar.

Future design should use real non-software falsification cases rather than
generic examples. Useful corpora include design and organizational research and
production research-and-report work spanning assignment management,
authoritative research, evidence-backed writing, human review, and exceptional
document acquisition or communications.

Current must eventually support responsibility-scoped skill loading,
non-engineering organizations without workflow engines, linked native Work
records, one shared organization model, and visible organization reshaping.
Those product capabilities enable the roadmap but do not authorize Throughline
to implement routing or workflow state.

## How Progress Is Judged

Primary measure: **supported meaning retained per 1,000 tokens**, paired with
reader comprehension.

A frozen source packet supplies an adjudicated inventory of requirements,
counterexamples, uncertainty, and prohibited claims. An output fails if it
loses an approved item or invents intent, regardless of length. A blinded reader
then identifies the promised reality, open decision, and restoring condition.
Only after those gates pass do token count and comprehension time matter.

No comparative efficacy percentage is claimed until fixtures, raw runs, reader
protocol, scoring method, and reproduction steps are public.

## Non-Goals

- "Talk to me like I am five" or reductive deletion.
- Uniformly short messages, vocabulary policing, or a title-length validator.
- Dropping approved capability, uncertainty, counterexamples, or evidence.
- Deciding proof sufficiency, Mystery causality, Git procedure, or runtime UI.
- Turning grammar into routing authority, a workflow engine, or hidden state.
- Claiming the current software-shaped grammar is already universal.

## Contributing

Useful contributions provide a source packet, an explicit meaning inventory, a
candidate expression, and evidence that independent readers understand it with
no lost or invented intent. Domain work should identify which vocabulary,
artifacts, stages, and evidence rules genuinely change decisions. Style
preferences without a comprehension or fidelity effect are not enough.

## Direction Status

The v0.1 public release preserves accepted Throughline behavior. Its only
changes from the version used inside Current are a packaging-only
reference-path correction and the replacement of internal names, ticket IDs,
and file references with generic role language; no rule changed. The corrected
layout passed packaging proof before those wording changes. Generalization, specialization, file splitting, and
meaning-compression behavior remain post-release Stories, not v0.1 changes.
